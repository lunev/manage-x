No login required.

1. Open the popup from the toolbar icon.
2. In Extensions, hover an icon, open its ⋮ menu > Add rule. Under Enabled URLs enter *.youtube.com, save.
3. Open youtube.com in a new tab: the extension turns on.
4. Click an icon to toggle it directly (rule-controlled icons can't be).
5. Groups: click +, pick 2+ extensions, add a URL, save.

Permissions: storage (rules, synced by Chrome Sync), management (toggle extensions), tabs (active tab URL). No network requests.
